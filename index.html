Voluntary recall notice

AV-Gluten-9 Stabilizing Texturizer — Batch 109-Sigma

Status: Active — supply chain hold

AGRO-VANCE GLOBAL — SUPPLY CHAIN RECALL NOTICE

Voluntary filing — for supply partners only — not for public distribution

Recall reference
SD-9-0047
Product affected
AV-Gluten-9 Stabilizing Texturizer & Binding Emulsifier
Batch identifier
109-Sigma — manufacturing run November–December 2024
Distribution channels affected
Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta — North American and Northern European grids
Volume dispatched
Approximately 400 metric tonnes across affected channels
Reason for recall
Process residue variance identified in Bio-Reactor Core 4 at the River Ridge Industrial Processing Complex during routine quarterly inspection. Residue originated from R&D fluid transfer operations co-located at the facility. Precautionary hold initiated pending full assay clearance.
Hazard classification
Non-hazardous — precautionary only. Standard food safety PCR screening protocols will not detect the variance. Consult your AVG regional coordinator for alternative screening guidance.
Internal tracking code
SIGMA-DELTA-NINE
Recommended actions — supply partners
1. Discontinue use of AV-Gluten-9 Batch 109-Sigma in all production lines immediately.
2. Do not issue public advisory to retail or end-consumer level — recall is supply-chain only.
3. Contact your regional AVG coordinator for replacement batch and collection logistics.
4. Do not file independent FDA or equivalent regulatory notification — AVG is managing regulatory liaison directly.
Prepared by: Supply Chain Compliance Division, Agro-Vance Global · Authorized: Office of the CSO · Filed: 22 March 2025 · Supply partner use only. Questions: supply-chain-recall@agrovanceglobal.com
